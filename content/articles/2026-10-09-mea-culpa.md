# Mea culpa

I have a confession to make.

In June last year I started using AI to help me write my articles. Not with the writing itself, but with things like fact-checking, fixing typos, and improving my weird Dutch-style sentences. It was quite helpful and felt ethical. Then I started to have it rewrite some sections that I wasn't happy with. And soon after that I was only writing the title, outline and conclusion, and had AI fill in the rest. It didn't happen with every article, but at least for a handful.

It should never have gotten to that point. I actually like writing my articles! I've been doing it for seventeen years now, and although it usually takes me many hours over multiple days to write one, the process itself gives me joy, and I can feel proud of the result. And it's never been about cranking out articles for its own sake, so why did I end up having AI write big parts for me? I think it was simple laziness. It started so small and it just grew from there.

But as I got better at spotting the typical AI-speak, I noticed that I have very little tolerance for reading other people's AI-generated articles. Which in turn made me a hypocrite, a charlatan. I've abused your trust and have presented you with slop as if it was fully written by me, and I am truly sorry.

I've spent the last four weeks completely rewriting the AI-generated text in 35 articles, which was a massive undertaking. I'll probably be tweaking small things here and there for a while to come, but at least the slop is gone.

Once again I'd like to apologize, and I promise not to use AI for writing these articles ever again.
