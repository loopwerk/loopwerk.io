# My dream meal
You know [Off Menu](https://www.offmenupodcast.co.uk), the podcast where a guest chooses their dream meal in the dream restaurant? Here's my menu.

## Still or sparkling water
Still, from the Oeschinen lake near Kandersteg (Switzerland). They sell it for 4 euros per bottle which is insane, but it's the best water I've ever had.

## Poppadoms or bread
Some freshly baked bread with olive oil and salt would be nice. A good crust and a fluffy inside please, and no seeds or nuts. Or, as a hack to get one of my favorite dishes into the menu: Vietnamese bahn mi with chicken. Specifically the one from [The Base](https://thebase.berlin), in Berlin (Germany). That was the best sandwich I've ever had in my life. 

## Starter
Spicy (but not too spicy) Korean fried chicken. I also like karaage chicken, but the Korean version is usually more flavorful. 

## Main course
A big bowl of noodle soup with chicken from [Noodle Station](https://noodlestation.is) in Reykjavik (Iceland). Specifically from their old location at Skólavörðustígur, circa 2014, when I lived around the corner. The most amazing food, I'd eat it twice a week. After I moved back to the Netherlands I had recurring dreams about this soup, it haunted me.

Honorable munchion: Tonkotsu ramen from Tensai Ramen in Rotterdam (Netherlands). With extra egg and extra pork. Not quite the same nostalgia as Noodle Station, and it never haunted my dreams, but possibly an even better soup.

The main course was actually really hard to choose because I also absolutely love tacos, Thai curry, and hamburgers. But a good bowl of ramen feeds my soul like nothing else can.

## Side dish
Normally I wouldn't have a side dish with my soup, and I'd have no space for it with the bread, starter and dessert anyway. But this is the dream restaurant where you can't be too full, so I'll choose the BBQ ribs tacos from a small tapas restaurant in Terschelling (Netherlands) called [El Mundo](https://elmundo-terschelling.com).

## Drink
I don't drink alcohol, but I do love a good 0% wheat beer, especially the German ones (Weihenstephaner, Franziskaner, Paulaner). I also adore [ApfelSinfonie](https://www.manufaktur-joerg-geiger.de/en/Meadow-Apples-Symphony/208-3) from Manufaktur Jörg Geiger. It's an alchohol-free sparkling wine, often used by fancy restaurants to pair with the food. But since this is the dream restaurant I'll use the genie to get something that I've had once and could never find again: tarragon-ginger soda from Wostok.

## Dessert
You know sticky rice with mango? That, but replace the mango with banana boiled in coconut milk (kluai buat chi). I first had this combination in a small Thai restaurant called Thung Thong in Lindau (Germany). I've always been a fan of the sticky rice, but the mango not so much. Having it with the banana instead blew me away.

Honorable munchion: my first ever proper fancy 6 course dinner was in restaurant [Passio](http://passiodining.fi), in Helsinki (Finland). Everything was to die for but especially the dessert, which was birch ice cream. Oh my god, this was amazing. Actually, just add a big scoop of that to my dessert!
