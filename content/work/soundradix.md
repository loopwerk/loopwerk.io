# Sound Radix

I took over end-to-end development of [soundradix.com](https://www.soundradix.com), consisting of a Django backend with a SvelteKit frontend. I replaced the entire Django Oscar webshop with a custom-built system, unlocking features that were previously impossible, significantly improving performance, and measurably increasing yearly revenue. I also redesigned the shop administration and reporting tools, making sales insights more accessible and cutting operational overhead, rewrote the frontend from JavaScript to TypeScript, and added comprehensive test coverage.
