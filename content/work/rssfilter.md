# RSSfilter.com

Tired of seeing the same topics over and over in your RSS reader? My project [rssfilter.com](https://rssfilter.com) lets you fine-tune your feeds by removing the noise. Just enter any public RSS feed URL and add the keywords or categories you'd like to filter out. It'll give you a clean, filtered version of that feed, so you only see the content that matters to you.
